Core Design Foundation

Engineered Around 3 Key Design Goals

Every screen, gauge, and algorithmic pipeline is purposefully built to address cybersecurity operational friction points.

01
HMW Goal 1

Unified Context View

Eliminates "swivel-chair fatigue" by combining the current 0–100 risk score, a 30-day circadian behavior trend curve, and raw activity logs on a single view.

• Interactive 30-day SVG date scrubbing
• Peer group baseline comparison indicator
• Time-correlated telemetry with zero tabs
Screen: Unified Dashboard View
02
HMW Goal 2

AI with Human Oversight

Machine learning proposes candidate flags but never auto-closes or escalates. Every alert requires explicit human action with a feedback loop.

• "Why was this flagged?" corroboration panel
• Confirm, Dismiss, or Review actions
• Auditable SQLite feedback dataset
Screen: Human Oversight & Feedback
03
HMW Goal 3

Smarter Alert Prioritization

Transforms overwhelming alert queues into auto-grouped incidents prioritized by multi-variable risk math and role-based baseline deviation.

• 1.35x multiplier for privileged accounts
• Co-occurring signals bonus (+26 max)
• Adaptive per-role vs global volume limits

Operational Lifecycle

How UEBA Sentinel Detects & Corroborates Threats

01

Ingestion & Baselines

Ingests multi-source authentication, file access, and network logs. Computes typical hours and daily volume baselines per departmental role.

02

Isolation Forest Scoring

Extracts 6D feature vectors. An ensemble of 60 isolation trees scores multivariate anomalies without rigid static rule thresholds.

03

Threat Corroboration

Groups related session events into attack stages (Ingress, Privilege, Exfiltration) to produce a single prioritized incident ticket.

04

Analyst Feedback Loop

Human analysts confirm or dismiss with notes. Decisions are recorded in SQLite to evaluate accuracy and adjust future baseline tolerances.
